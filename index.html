About Me

My work sits at the intersection of maritime systems, data analytics, and sustainable ocean development. I study how quantitative models and field-based evidence can improve port forecasting, explain the livelihood transitions of coastal communities, and support safer offshore wind planning.

Across these topics, I combine modelling with practical maritime knowledge. My methods range from GM–Markov forecasting and optimization to interviews, questionnaires, multivariate analysis, AIS trajectory processing, GIS, and spatial decision-making.

Project 01

Cargo Throughput Forecasting

Forecasting Systems

GM(1,1) · Markov Residual Correction · CPSO · Forecast Evaluation

This project develops an integrated GM–Markov–CPSO framework for short-term cargo throughput forecasting at Huilai Port. The grey model captures the underlying growth trend, Markov residual correction responds to random fluctuations, and CPSO improves parameter selection. The resulting model achieved a MAPE of 8.06%, providing a more stable basis for port planning, berth allocation, operational scheduling, and resource decisions.

Project 02

Fishermen Livelihoods & Offshore Wind Development

Coastal Transition

Mixed Methods · Grounded Theory · PCA & K-means · SWOT–PEST

This mixed-methods study examines how offshore wind construction reshapes fishermen's livelihoods in Huilai County. Interviews and grounded-theory coding were integrated with questionnaire analysis, factor analysis, PCA, and clustering to identify distinct transition attitudes and constraints. Comparative cases and SWOT–PEST analysis then informed four practical pathways spanning offshore services, marine ranching, coastal tourism, and non-fishing employment, supported by collaborative governance.

Project 03

Offshore Wind Farm Site Selection Optimization

Maritime Spatial Planning

AIS Trajectories · Image Processing · K-means · GIS · Spatial Optimization

This project uses AIS trajectory-density imagery to identify habitual shipping routes and translate them into geographic constraints for offshore wind farm siting. Image preprocessing, K-means clustering, edge extraction, and Mercator coordinate transformation convert route patterns into GIS-ready boundaries. The resulting framework identifies candidate zones away from high-density traffic and supports transparent, safety-oriented spatial planning that can also incorporate water depth, wind resources, and environmental constraints.
